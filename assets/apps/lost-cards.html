The Lost Cards

A missing-data mystery: 30 students turned in survey cards, and some got lost. Why?

How it works

  1. Every student answered one question on a card: "How many hours did you study for this class last week?"
  2. Some cards went missing according to a hidden rule. Face-down cards are lost; we know where the student sat, but not their answer.
  3. Use the clues below to guess the rule, then reveal the truth.

Discussion questions
  1. Before the reveal: is the observed average probably too high, too low, or about right? What makes you think so?
  2. What does the "lost by seat" chart tell you? What can it never tell you?
  3. Averaging within each row and then combining fixes the estimate for one rule but not another. Which one, and why?
  4. Think of a real dataset with missing values. Which of the three stories fits it best?
